What are Stock Products?

Stock products are standardized items that are kept in inventory and are ready for immediate sale or distribution. Unlike custom-made or "on-demand" items, stock products are manufactured or purchased in advance based on forecasted demand.

How to create stock products in SPS

Login to your sps account

Go to the lists tab

Click on the plus "+" sign.

A form for product will open

Fill information regarding the product.

Click on the "Add New Product" button located on the bottom-right corner of the form

An overview of product will appear. The product now has been saved in the SPS

Searching saved Stock Products

Login to your sps account

Go to the lists tab

Click on the "Stock" button

Search for the product using its name or other filter options that are available

The product is visible.

Updating Stock Products

After searching the product you want to save, click on it. The overview of product will appear

Click on the update button in the upper-right corner of the window

The update product tab will appear, which allows to edit all the information regarding the product

After changing the values according to requirement, click on the update product on the bottom-right corner of the form

Changes will be saved

Deleting Stock Products

Go to the overview page of the product you want to delete(see above)

Click on the delete button on the upper-right corner of the page

This window will appear asking for confirmation.Again click Delete

The product is deleted
