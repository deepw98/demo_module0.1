What are Stock Products?

Stock products are standardized items that are kept in inventory and are ready for immediate sale or distribution. Unlike custom-made or "on-demand" items, stock products are manufactured or purchased in advance based on forecasted demand.

How to create stock products in SPS

Step 1:Login to your sps account

Step 2:Go to the lists tab

Step 3:Click on the plus "+" sign.

Step 4:A form for product will open

Step 5:Fill information regarding the product.

Step 6:Click on the "Add New Product" button located on the bottom-right corner of the form

Step 7:An overview of product will appear. The product now has been saved in the SPS

Searching saved Stock Products

Step 1:Login to your sps account

Step 2:Go to the lists tab

Step 3:Click on the "Stock" button

Step 4:Search for the product using its name or other filter options that are available

Step 5:The product is visible.

Updating Stock Products

Step 1:After searching the product you want to save, click on it. The overview of product will appear

Step 2:Click on the update button in the upper-right corner of the window

Step 3:The update product tab will appear, which allows to edit all the information regarding the product

Step 4:After changing the values according to requirement, click on the update product on the bottom-right corner of the form

Step 5:Changes will be saved

Deleting Stock Products

Step 1:Go to the overview page of the product you want to delete(see above)

Step 2:Click on the delete button on the upper-right corner of the jpgage

Step 3:This window will appear asking for confirmation.Again click Delete

Step 4:The product is deleted
